Ban' Yuh Belly — the story behind the app

"Ban' yuh belly" is a Caribbean saying — if you grew up in Trinidad, Tobago, Guyana, or Grenada, you know exactly what it means: brace yourself, tighten up, prepare for hard times ahead. The kind of thing a grandmother would say when money was scarce and sacrifice was necessary. This app was built so a household never has to hear those words unprepared again — when you know where every dollar is going, hard times stop catching you off guard. It's a passion project, built for the author's own family first.

What it does

  • Envelope budgeting Every expense category is an envelope with a monthly fill amount and its own running balance.
  • Integer-cent accounting Money is stored and calculated in integer cents, so balances don't drift from floating-point rounding.
  • Multiple household users Everyone in the household signs in with their own password.
  • Recurring bills & income Weekly, fortnightly, or monthly rules that post with one click and advance their own due date.
  • Bank-statement CSV import Bring in transactions from a bank export instead of typing each one in by hand.
  • Reports Spending distribution, month-over-month category trends, and budget history.
  • Savings targets Accumulating envelopes can carry a target amount and due date toward a goal.
  • Automated backups Scheduled, verified backups of the household's data.
  • Save-conflict protection If two people edit at the same time, changes aren't silently overwritten.
  • Self-hosted Runs on your own hardware via Docker or TrueNAS SCALE — JSON files on disk, no subscription, no bank-account linking.
